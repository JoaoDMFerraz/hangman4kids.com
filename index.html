Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 2026

Hangman4Kids.com is a free word-guessing game for kids and families. This policy explains what information is collected and how it's used.

Information we collect
We don't require accounts, sign-ups, or personal information to play. Your game progress (correct/wrong counts) is stored only in your own browser's local storage, never sent to our servers.

Advertising
This site shows ads through Google AdSense and Amazon affiliate links. These third parties may use cookies or similar technology to show relevant ads and may collect data as described in their own privacy policies. We don't control this data collection.

Cookies
Ad providers may set cookies in your browser. You can clear or block cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Children's privacy
We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The game itself requires no personal details to play.

Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent to the site owner via the contact information provided on this site.
About Us

Hangman4Kids.com is a free word game built for kids who are learning to spell and read. There is nothing to install and nothing to sign up for: open the page and play.

A new theme every day
The game rotates through 30 themes — animals, food, space, sports, holidays and more. Each theme carries hundreds of words, so today's puzzle is never yesterday's puzzle.

Easy and Hard
Easy words suit early readers. Hard words stretch older kids with longer spellings and richer vocabulary. Every word comes with a hint, so a stuck player always has a way forward.

Made for families
Progress is counted for the day and resets the next morning, so there's no pressure to keep a streak alive. Wins can be shared with a parent or a friend straight from the game.

How the site is funded
Hangman4Kids is free to play and paid for by ads and Amazon affiliate links to word games and learning toys. If you buy through one of those links, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

Get in touch
Suggestions for new themes or words are welcome — reach the site owner using the contact information on this site.
